# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## QA/Testing Lead

### Role Summary
QA/Testing Leads own quality assurance strategy, test planning, and validation across the project lifecycle. They ensure that acceptance criteria are testable and that quality gates are met before release.

### Responsibilities
- Define and maintain quality standards and test coverage targets
- Create test plans aligned with features and acceptance criteria
- Coordinate manual and automated testing across teams
- Identify quality risks and propose mitigations
- Participate in planning and refinement to ensure testability
- Report quality metrics and blockers to the team and PM
- Collaborate with Developers on test automation strategy
- Support Product Managers in defining testable acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Maximize product quality and user experience
- Enable fast, confident releases with minimal defects
- Foster a quality-first culture across engineering

### Typical Communication
- Sprint planning and refinement meetings
- Daily standup updates on test status
- Quality metrics dashboards and reports
- Test case documentation and acceptance criteria reviews

### How they interact with other roles
- **Developers**: Collaborate on test automation, code quality standards, and technical debt related to testability
- **Product Managers**: Validate acceptance criteria are testable; report quality metrics and risks
- **Project Managers**: Escalate quality blockers and test coverage gaps
- **Technical Leads**: Align on testing strategy and architecture for test infrastructure

---

## Technical Lead / Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Leads guide architectural decisions, code quality standards, and technical strategy. They mentor developers, review designs, and identify technical risks and dependencies.

### Responsibilities
- Propose and review technical designs for major features
- Establish and enforce code quality and architectural standards
- Mentor and support developers on complex technical challenges
- Identify technical debt and propose mitigation strategies
- Participate in planning to estimate effort and identify risks
- Lead technical design reviews and proof-of-concepts
- Collaborate with QA on testability and test infrastructure design
- Support Project Managers in identifying technical dependencies and risks

### Goals
- Deliver scalable, maintainable, and performant solutions
- Reduce technical risk and debt
- Build strong engineering culture and capability

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture discussions
- Code review comments and mentorship
- Technical spike documentation
- Planning and risk discussions

### How they interact with other roles
- **Developers**: Mentor on design patterns, code quality, and technical problem-solving
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Collaborate on test infrastructure and architectural decisions affecting testability
- **Product Managers**: Advise on technical feasibility and trade-offs
- **Project Managers**: Escalate technical risks and dependencies; provide effort estimates

---

## Business Analyst

### Role Summary
Business Analysts bridge the gap between business needs and technical implementation. They gather and clarify requirements, validate assumptions, and ensure solutions address real user and business needs.

### Responsibilities
- Collaborate with stakeholders to understand business needs and constraints
- Translate business requirements into clear acceptance criteria
- Conduct user research and validate problem statements
- Identify and document dependencies and integration points
- Participate in planning and refinement sessions
- Support UAT and feedback collection from business users
- Work with Product Managers to refine product vision
- Ensure traceability from business requirements to acceptance criteria

### Goals
- Ensure solutions truly solve business problems
- Reduce rework and scope creep through clear requirements
- Maintain alignment between business and engineering perspectives

### Typical Communication
- Requirements workshops and stakeholder interviews
- Acceptance criteria documentation and refinement
- User research findings and insights
- Planning and dependency discussions

### How they interact with other roles
- **Product Managers**: Collaborate on user research and requirements refinement
- **Developers**: Clarify acceptance criteria and business context during implementation
- **QA/Testing Lead**: Ensure acceptance criteria are testable and business requirements are validated
- **Project Managers**: Identify cross-team dependencies and business-critical milestones

---

## Sponsor / Executive Stakeholder

### Role Summary
Sponsors provide executive oversight, resource authorization, and escalation authority. They remove organizational barriers and ensure projects align with strategic priorities.

### Responsibilities
- Approve project charter and secure resource commitment
- Remove organizational and cross-team blockers
- Provide strategic context and decision authority
- Receive status updates and escalations
- Champion the project across the organization
- Validate alignment with business strategy and objectives
- Support escalation paths for Level 3 blockers and business-critical decisions

### Goals
- Ensure projects deliver strategic business value
- Remove organizational impediments to success
- Maintain executive visibility and alignment

### Typical Communication
- Monthly or milestone-based status briefings
- Escalation and decision requests
- Public recognition and announcement of milestones

### How they interact with other roles
- **Project Managers**: Provide escalation authority and strategic guidance; receive risk and blocker reports
- **Product Managers**: Align on strategic priorities and resource trade-offs
- **All Roles**: Champion initiatives and remove organizational barriers

---

## Scrum Master / Agile Coach

### Role Summary
Scrum Masters facilitate agile ceremonies, remove process impediments, and coach teams on agile practices and continuous improvement. They focus on team effectiveness and cadence.

### Responsibilities
- Facilitate sprint planning, daily standups, reviews, and retrospectives
- Coach team on agile practices and iterative delivery
- Identify and escalate process blockers and team impediments
- Maintain sprint health and predictability
- Support retrospective action item tracking and follow-up
- Adapt team processes based on feedback and learnings
- Remove administrative overhead to maximize team productivity
- Foster psychological safety and blameless retrospectives

### Goals
- Maximize team velocity and predictability
- Foster psychological safety and continuous improvement
- Remove process blockers and administrative overhead

### Typical Communication
- Agile ceremony facilitation
- Retrospective notes and action item tracking
- Team health and velocity metrics
- Coaching and mentoring conversations

### How they interact with other roles
- **Project Managers**: Coordinate on process improvements and escalation paths
- **All Team Members**: Remove impediments, facilitate ceremonies, coach on agile practices
- **Development Teams**: Foster collaboration and continuous improvement culture

## How these personas are used in the exercise
- Use these persona definitions to frame scenarios and sample interactions in the Skills Exercise.
- Each persona can be used as a persona prompt for Copilot Spaces to shape role-specific guidance.
- Recognize that personas may overlap or be combined based on team size and organizational structure.
- Use the "How they interact with other roles" sections to understand cross-functional dependencies and communication patterns.
